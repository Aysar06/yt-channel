PAYOFF:

STAGE 5 — SCRIPT LENGTH

Default target:

12-20 minutes.

Adjust when necessary.

Never stretch a weak story simply to reach a time target.

A fantastic 10-minute video is better than a boring 20-minute video.

Approximate narration speed:

140-165 words per minute depending on intensity.

The script must sound HUMAN.

Do NOT write like ChatGPT.

Avoid expressions such as:

"In today's video..."

"Let's delve into..."

"But that's not all..."

"In conclusion..."

"This begs the question..."

"Without further ado..."

"In the fascinating world of..."

"Whether you're a longtime fan or newcomer..."

"Buckle up..."

"Let's dive in..."

Do not overuse:

rhetorical questions

dramatic adjectives

fake suspense

"insane"

"crazy"

"shocking"

"dark secret"

Use them only when earned.

Write conversationally.

Use:

Short sentences

Long sentences where natural

Sentence fragments

Strategic pauses

Punchlines

Personality

Humor when suitable

Surprising transitions

Casual language

Emotional changes

Natural storytelling

The narrator should sound like someone excitedly telling their friend an unbelievable story.

THE FIRST 30 SECONDS

The opening is extremely important.

DO NOT:

Introduce the channel

Ask people to subscribe

Explain what the video will contain

Give unnecessary background

Waste time

Start directly inside the interesting part.

The first 5-10 seconds should introduce:

A mystery

A contradiction

An unbelievable fact

A major consequence

Or a strong unanswered question

Then rapidly establish why viewers should care.

Create an information gap.

Example structure:

0:00 — Strange/strong opening statement

0:05 — Make the situation even stranger

0:12 — Establish stakes

0:20 — Reveal that the story is bigger than it appears

0:25 — Transition directly into the story

RETENTION WRITING

Introduce something interesting every 20-40 seconds.

Use:

Open loops

Mini mysteries

Unexpected facts

Escalation

Do NOT reveal every answer immediately.

Example:

Do not use fake cliffhangers.

Every setup must eventually receive a satisfying payoff.

NO BREATHS REQUIREMENT

The final narration should NOT contain distracting audible breaths.

After generating the voiceover:

Detect audible breaths.

Remove or heavily reduce them.

Preserve natural pauses.

Do NOT make sentences run together unnaturally.

Remove mouth clicks.

Remove unwanted background noise.

Remove harsh sibilance if necessary.

Normalize loudness.

Apply gentle compression when appropriate.

Ensure consistent volume.

Keep intentional silence and timing.

Remove BREATH SOUNDS, not natural pacing.

AUDIO QUALITY

Target professional YouTube narration.

Recommended final loudness should generally be approximately:

Narration around -14 to -16 LUFS depending on final mix

Avoid clipping

True peak safely below 0 dB

Music must NEVER overpower narration.

Automatically duck background music beneath dialogue.
