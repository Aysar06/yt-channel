# P.T. production design

Concept: a familiar hallway becomes an archive that cannot reliably be reopened.

1920×1080 at 24 fps. Grounded, cinematic horror-documentary typography and archival objects. Palette: warm bone #F2EBD9, olive black #11150F, electric red #E84C3D, muted olive #798568, deep ink #0A0E0B. One strong accent per frame.

Type: League Gothic 400 for forceful short titles; IBM Plex Mono 400/700 for dates, names and archive captions. Headline 100–180px. Labels 24–30px. Short body 36–44px. Keep 90px title-safe sides and 72px top; captions reserved below y=870.

Focal elements: a door, a verified screenshot detail, an installation tile, a date, a changing loop diagram. Edge anchors identify chapter and source. Source receipts are designed quotations/paraphrases, never counterfeit article screenshots. Authentic images credited to their publishing source; original art labeled ILLUSTRATION.

Shot grammar: authentic full-bleed gameplay image, asymmetrical crop of a real post or photograph, oversized word on bone, thin overhead hallway diagram, timeline pinned to a date, source receipt, two-state access comparison, original camera diagram. Prioritize sourced visuals from official sources, contemporary reports and public community posts. User correction on 2026-09-07: use far less AI imagery. Target zero AI images; do not fake evidence or repeat an invented hallway as filler. Cut or change the visual action every 2–8s; reshape the entire composition around each 20–40s paragraph. Vary framing in consecutive shots.

Motion: multi-phase-camera (slow settle/push and small drift), svg-path-draw (loop/camera/download paths), kinetic-beat-slam (three short stakes words on a shared beat). Hard cuts carry most changes; brief image dissolves for reflective transitions. No constant shake, no gratuitous glitches, no huge word-for-word captions.

Audio: Andrew neural English narration tested against Guy; select after sample assessment. Subtle varied rate by performance cue. Three evolving licensed Scott Buckley music moods, ducked beneath speech. Purposeful short click, impact and reverse swell at chapter turns, with silence before the late camera reveal.

Five concept directions evaluated internally: (1) hallway-driven investigation; (2) last console as museum exhibit; (3) download button as disappearing doorway; (4) a storage-deletion checklist that becomes a thriller; (5) a letter from the game's last installed copy. Select (1) with the physical archive metaphor of (2), because it naturally ties verified gameplay evidence to the availability story. Avoid inventing a lone 'last copy' or treating the cancelled larger game as finished.
