A free horror game became something players were afraid to delete. The strange story of P.T. is about what happens when the hallway survives, but the way back disappears.

From its disguised 2014 launch to Silent Hills' cancellation, the loss of normal re-download access, and Lance McDonald's camera discovery: this is the story of the teaser people actually played. Existing installed copies were not all remotely erased; P.T. and the cancelled Silent Hills are distinct projects.
