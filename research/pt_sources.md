# P.T. / Silent Hills — verified source ledger

Research date: 2026-09-06. This is a factual ledger for original narration, not a script. All summaries below are paraphrases except short explicitly marked quotations. Publication dates matter: historical reports establish what happened then, not every present-day workaround. Core editorial distinction: **P.T. was a released playable teaser; Silent Hills was the cancelled project it advertised.**

## Editorial guardrails

- Strong central question: How did a free advertisement become a piece of gaming history that people were afraid to delete?
- 'The Horror Game You Were Never Supposed to Lose' works as an emotional/metaphorical title, not a claim of developer intent. Clearer literal option: 'The Horror Game You Couldn't Download Again'.
- Do not say Konami remotely erased installed copies, that the original is completely lost, or that no preservation/reconstruction exists.
- Do not claim the completed Silent Hills was destroyed. It was an early project; P.T. was a teaser with an explicit content disclaimer.
- No invented development budget, cancellation motive, employee animus, purchase price, resale value, or exact current install count.
- Avoid claiming P.T. caused Resident Evil 7's first-person direction; that requires separate developer evidence and chronology.
- Do not present fan lore interpretations as settled explanation, including Kojima allegedly encoding his dispute with Konami into the story.
- The Lisa reveal is a good late-story factual payoff, but needs a labeled original diagram or licensed/brief contextual evidence. An invented corridor should be labeled illustration/reconstruction.
